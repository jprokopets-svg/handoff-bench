# BRIEFING: max_area_container.py

**GOAL:**
Implement a solution to find the maximum area of water that can be contained between two vertical lines (classic "Container With Most Water" problem).

**STATE:**
- Two-pointer algorithm implemented in `max_area()` function
- Algorithm starts with pointers at both ends and moves inward
- Calculates area as `width × min(left_height, right_height)` at each step
- Moves the pointer pointing to the shorter line inward to explore potentially larger areas
